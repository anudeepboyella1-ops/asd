✦ Process

How It Works

Three simple steps to professional subtitles

01

Upload Video

Drag and drop or upload your video file. Supports MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, and WebM up to 2GB. Files are securely stored in Amazon S3 via AWS Amplify.

Amazon S3 + Amplify Storage
02

AI Transcription

AWS Transcribe automatically extracts speech from the audio track, generating timestamped subtitles with speaker detection and punctuation.

AWS Transcribe (Streaming / Batch)
03

Translate & Export

Select your target language and AWS Translate generates accurate subtitles. Download in SRT, VTT or any other format instantly.

AWS Translate + Export Engine
